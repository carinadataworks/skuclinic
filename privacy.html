Privacy

All product, order, cost, and ad data is processed locally in your browser. It is never uploaded to us and we never store it.

SkuClinic is a static site published by Kyle Guy. There is no account system and no server that receives your spreadsheets.

What stays on your device

When you drop a file into the app, the file is read by JavaScript running in your browser. The numbers are calculated there. Uploaded files and calculated results are never sent.

If you use a paid plan’s “saved settings,” the workspace is written to this browser’s local storage on this device. We cannot see it. Clearing site data for this site deletes it. It does not follow you to another computer.

An unlock code, if you enter one, is stored only in that local storage. It is not an account.

What you type

Only what you type into the signup form and the feedback form is sent: your email, if you give one, and your answers. That message goes through FormSubmit to the site owner’s inbox. Uploaded files and results are never included.

If the signup address is not configured, the form does not send anything. It tells you that nothing is stored.

Payments

Billing is not on yet. Pro and Agency buttons join the founding list and do not charge a card. When checkout is on, those buttons open Stripe. SkuClinic does not receive your card number.

Page views

When Cloudflare Web Analytics is turned on, the site loads a cookieless counter from Cloudflare that records page views. It does not receive your files or your answers. When it is off, that script is not loaded.

Cookies

SkuClinic does not set tracking cookies. The optional page-view counter is cookieless.
