Privacy

All product, order, cost, and ad data is processed locally in your browser. It is never uploaded to us and we never store it.

TrueMargin is a static site published by Kyle Guy. There is no account system and no server that receives your spreadsheets.

What stays on your device

When you drop a file into the app, the file is read by JavaScript running in your browser. The numbers are calculated there. They are not sent to TrueMargin, to a spreadsheet service, or to an analytics tool. This site loads no third-party scripts.

If you use a paid plan’s “saved settings,” the workspace is written to this browser’s local storage on this device. We cannot see it. Clearing site data for this site deletes it. It does not follow you to another computer.

An unlock code, if you enter one, is stored only in that local storage. It is not an account.

Email form

Email signup opens soon. Nothing is stored yet. Typing an address and pressing the button does not send or keep it.

Payments

Checkout opens soon. Until then, paid buttons say “Coming soon” and go nowhere. When checkout is on, those buttons open Stripe. TrueMargin does not receive your card number.

Cookies

TrueMargin does not set tracking cookies and does not run analytics.
