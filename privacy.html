Privacy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Your product, order, cost, fee, and ad data is processed locally in your browser. It is never uploaded to us, and we never store it.

SkuClinic is operated by Carina Dataworks. It is a static website: there is no account system and no server that receives your spreadsheets.

What stays on your device

When you drop a file into the app, JavaScript in your browser reads it and does the math there. Your files and calculated results are not sent to Carina Dataworks or anyone else.

The app keeps a few small items in this browser’s local storage on this device. We cannot see them:

  • Your workspace (products and settings), only on Pro or Agency, so it is there next time.
  • Column mappings you chose to remember for a file type.
  • That you agreed to the Terms, with the date and time you agreed, and which version.
  • Small preferences: your plan or unlock code, whether to show the welcome box and the next-steps tips.

Clearing site data for this site in your browser deletes all of it. It does not follow you to another computer or browser. Because we never receive it, we cannot recover it for you; export your fixes if you need a copy.

What you type into a form

Only what you type into the signup form and the feedback form is sent: your email, if you give one, and your answers. It goes through FormSubmit to the site owner’s inbox. Your files and results are never included. If the signup address is not configured, the form sends nothing and says so.

Payments

Billing is not on yet. Pro and Agency buttons join the founding list and do not charge a card. When checkout is on, those buttons will open Stripe, and SkuClinic will not receive your card number.

Page views

When Cloudflare Web Analytics is turned on, the site loads a cookieless counter from Cloudflare that records page views. It does not receive your files or your answers. When it is off, that script is not loaded.

Cookies

SkuClinic does not set tracking cookies. The optional page-view counter is cookieless.

Changes

If this page changes, the “Last updated” date above changes too.

Contact

Questions about this page: use the feedback form in the app. SkuClinic is operated by Carina Dataworks.
