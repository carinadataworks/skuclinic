Terms

All data you import is processed locally in your browser. It is never uploaded to us and we never store it.

TrueMargin is a calculator. It turns the files you give it into bid ceilings and a suggested action per SKU. It does not change your Shopify store or your Google Ads account. You decide what to do with the numbers.

The tool

Results depend on the files you import and on assumptions the app states (default profit buffer, which units count as sold, how a date range is annualized). They are not a promise of profit, and they are not ads-management advice.

Plans

The free plan analyzes up to 25 SKUs in the current browser tab. Pro is listed at $29 a month or $290 a year for unlimited SKUs, settings saved in this browser, and CSV export. Agency is listed at $79 a month for freelance ad managers and adds separate client stores on this device. Until the Stripe links in config.js are filled in, paid buttons say “Coming soon.” A preview unlock code is a temporary stand-in, checked only in the browser, and is not a subscription.

Your files

You keep your files. Because they are processed locally and are not uploaded, we do not have a copy to return, delete, or secure. If you save a workspace, it remains in your browser until you clear it.
