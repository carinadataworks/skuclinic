Terms

All data you import is processed locally in your browser. It is never uploaded to us and we never store it.

TrueMargin is a calculator. It turns the files you give it into bid ceilings and a suggested action per SKU. It does not change your Shopify store or your Google Ads account. You decide what to do with the numbers.

The tool

Results depend on the files you import and on assumptions the app states (default profit buffer, which units count as sold, how a date range is annualized). They are not a promise of profit, and they are not ads-management advice.
