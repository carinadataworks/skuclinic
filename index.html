ROAS hides the SKUs that don’t pay you.

Shopping and Performance Max chase conversions. They do not know that the candle keeps a dollar and the bottle keeps eighteen. The account can look healthy while a handful of products eat the payout. Cost lives in Shopify. Spend lives in Google. Almost nothing puts them on the same row and tells you what to change.

What you get

A bid that respects margin

Max profitable CPC and a target ROAS from price minus product cost, not from revenue. Thin click counts are marked low confidence.

Profit Leak Finder

True profit per unit and per SKU after cost, shipping, ad spend, and — if you sell there too — Amazon fees. Losses roll up to an estimated annual leak.

One action, written out

Raise price, cut ads, stop selling, or review fees. Each row says why, in one sentence.

Files you already have

Shopify, Etsy, Google Ads, Merchant Center, and Amazon exports, a cost sheet, or the SkuClinic template. No file? Type a product into Quick profit. Columns are detected, then you check them.

How it works

SkuClinic finds the header row, names the file, and shows which column maps where. Change anything that looks wrong. Title rows and a totals row are skipped.

Each SKU gets a max CPC and, when the math is negative, one action. Export the list when you are on Pro.

Questions

Do I connect Shopify or Google Ads?

No. You export a CSV and drop it here. That is the whole integration. It also means a freelancer can work from a file a client sent, without access to the ad account.

Who is it for?

Shopify brands running Google Shopping or Performance Max, roughly $10K–$200K a month in sales, with about 20–300 SKUs. If you have three products, a spreadsheet is enough. If you have 5,000, you want a full bidding suite.

What files do I need?

A Shopify product export and a Google Ads product report. Add a Shopify orders export if you want real units sold, and a cost sheet if Cost per item is blank. Amazon fee preview or a settlement file is optional, for brands that also sell there. Steps are in the export guide. Samples are in the app.

What happens after 25 SKUs on the free plan?

The app keeps the first 25 and tells you how many were left out. Pro, at the planned founding price and not billed yet, raises the cap to 50,000 SKUs, saves the workspace in this browser, and turns on CSV export.

Can I pay for a plan today?

No. Planned pricing: the Audit Pass is $29 once for 30 days of Pro. Pro is $15 a month or $120 a year. Pro and the Audit Pass cover up to 50,000 SKUs. Multichannel is $29 a month or $240 a year and adds up to 5 stores or clients, up to 50,000 SKUs per store. Founding members, the first 200 paid accounts, get 40% off for life on Pro and Multichannel while the subscription stays active. Nothing is billed yet. The buttons join the founding list.

Will my numbers show up on another computer?

No. Nothing is sent to us. If you save on Pro or Multichannel, it lives in this browser only. Clearing site data clears it.

Is this financial or ads advice?

No. It is arithmetic on the files you provide. You decide what to change in Google Ads.
